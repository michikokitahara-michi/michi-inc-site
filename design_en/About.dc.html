MESSAGE

Message

My strength is leading zero-to-one branding, rebranding and new value creation.

I draw out the aspirations of everyone involved with a brand, product or service, identify its strengths and sharply define the root of its value. Then, guided by a shared brand value and image, I align the goals and aspirations of every stakeholder, and walk alongside clients so they can manage strategy and execution that realize the brand purpose and strengthen its proposition.

I also bring deep knowledge of systematic frameworks — from tools that keep brand equity consistent to IMC planning — and a proven scheme for building organizational capability through education and on-the-job training. By making things visible and creating a shared language, branding stays consistent and independent of any one individual, even as the team grows.

Through the power of branding and marketing, I want to elevate Japan's finely crafted, delicate products and services into value — to bring back a world in which Japan is powerful and shines beautifully in the eyes of the world, and to contribute to the future of Japan's society and economy.

If you are interested, please feel free to reach out — even just to bounce ideas around.

Michiko Kitahara
