PHILOSOPHY

What We Believe

Creating value from zero to one is not drawing on a blank sheet of paper. Many dots are already there; when a purpose emerges, those dots connect and become value. That is what we believe.

ART & SCIENCE

Data holds only the past. Behind data, there is always a human mind. Beyond data lies the future.

ART — the right-brain world of sensibility, imagination and taste. SCIENCE — the left-brain world of logic and analysis. We believe that moving freely and in balance between the two is the key to innovation.

It is not simply that creative is ART and data is SCIENCE. Whether creating creative work or reading data, both perspectives — ART & SCIENCE — are essential.

Origin of Our Name

The name "MICHI" comes from our CEO's name, Michiko (規稚子). The first character of her name is taken from a word for "compass" that appears in the Mencius; the second, from the word for "young fish" — the young, still growing. Together, her name carries the meaning "one who shows the way to those striving to grow." In Japanese, michi also means "path."

Wishing to give back to society the many gifts she has received, and to help create paths yet unknown, she took part of her name for the company and founded MICHI inc. on January 23, 2025.

CONCEPTConcept

Before "how to sell," create "the core value."

"We can run efficient, data-driven marketing, but we can't create the new value that drives sustainable growth — and we lack people who can lead it." We hear this often.

MICHI inc. works with business owners to create not only the HOW (how to sell), but the WHAT (the core value).

When the core value is put into words and becomes a shared language inside and outside the company, everyone involved with the brand faces the same direction, thinks for themselves, and delivers a consistent brand experience. Unshaken by changes in people or departments, your brand escapes the "better" race over features and becomes one with a lasting reason to be chosen — achieving sustainable growth with a strong marketing organization that runs on its own.

This is grounded in experience leading zero-to-one value creation at a Japan-born global company and growing many brands. We weave ideas from your strengths, history and aspirations, and put a distinctive benefit into words. 01 — BRAND

Creating Brands

A brand is the "meaning" that forms in people's minds through layers of memory. However hard you think you are communicating the appeal of a product or service, if that meaning is vague, or differs from person to person and moment to moment, it is not a brand.

Precisely because data-driven, efficient marketing is now possible, it matters more than ever to connect every action to building "meaning" and to strengthening the brand.

We make visible the aspirations and sensibilities of each employee, and the tacit value of your products and services, then draw a consistent strategy that builds brand equity in line with your brand's purpose, proposition and personality — helping your brand grow to be loved by many, deeply and for a long time.

02 — EXPERIENCE

Delivering Brand Experiences

"We made a brand bible, but it lives only in PowerPoint." "Chasing short-term numbers like reach, CTR and CVR, our brand experience became just a 'push-to-buy' experience." We hear this often.

Yet as long as your audience is human, behavior will not move unless hearts do. MICHI inc. helps you build a "perception flow" — a blueprint for moving people. Anticipating a brand experience, feeling it, and wanting to tell others: we design every touchpoint to create that shift in perception.

From writing the right brief for agencies, to feedback grounded in the perception flow, to KPI design worked back from sales and built for review — we walk with you through every step of the PDCA cycle.

03 — ORGANIZATION

Growing Organizations Through Brands

Relying too heavily on data traps you in a "better" race over familiar features, and slowly wears the organization down.

Balancing data and sensibility, we advise on organization and process design that makes new value easier to create, and even on how marketers are evaluated — building organizations that can practice ART & SCIENCE marketing.

When to use which framework or data, how to imagine the consumer's heart behind it, and how to connect the dots and leap to new value — we work hands-on as part of your team to root marketing thinking in your organization.

We also run training and workshops on branding and value creation, growing teams — from executives to frontline members — who can speak about their brand's value in their own words and create new value.
